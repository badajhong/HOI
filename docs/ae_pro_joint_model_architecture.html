Task-Conditioned Joint IR–Depth Autoencoder

Architecture implemented in ae_pro_joint_train.py

Figure. The IR branch supplies an anchor representation of object-relative surface interaction. The student branch fuses a five-frame depth sequence with synchronized robot proprioception. Both branches are conditioned by the projected task-name feature c. Training aligns the depth posterior with the detached IR posterior and uses the shared decoder to reconstruct the original IR feature window.
